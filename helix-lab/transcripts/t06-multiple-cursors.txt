POSTSCRIPT: what the comma key keeps
$ hx numbers.txt; keys: C C (3/3 sels, primary on line 3), then ,
after C C:  NOR  numbers.txt  3/3 sels  3:1
after ,  :  NOR  numbers.txt  1 sel    3:1
The cursor stayed on line 3: comma keeps the primary selection
and dismisses the rest.
